 Emergency Room Wait Time Analysis

## Project Overview
Analysed 200 emergency room patient visits across shifts, days, triage levels and insurance types to uncover peak congestion patterns and operational bottlenecks.

**Industry:** Healthcare
**Tools:** MySQL + Microsoft Excel
**Level:** Intermediate
**Challenge:** Project 2 of 30-Day Data Analytics Challenge
---
## Problem Statement
Emergency rooms struggle with overcrowding and long wait times. This project uses data to answer:
- When are wait times longest?
- Which patients wait the most?
- Where should hospitals focus their resources?

---
## What I Did
- Built a dataset of 200 ER visits with 15 variables including shift, triage level, diagnosis and insurance type
- Wrote 8 SQL queries in MySQL to extract patterns across multiple dimensions
- Visualised findings in Excel with 4 dashboard charts

---
## Key Findings
- 🔴 Afternoon shifts average **129.9 mins** — 33% longer than morning (97.9 mins)
- 🔴 Non-Urgent patients wait **137.3 mins** vs just **18 mins** for Critical cases
- 🔴 Weekends are **21% worse** than weekdays — 130.9 vs 108.3 mins
- 🔴 Friday and Saturday are the most congested days of the week

---## Recommendation
Increase staffing during afternoon shift-change windows and consider redirecting non-urgent cases to off-peak hours to reduce overall wait times.
