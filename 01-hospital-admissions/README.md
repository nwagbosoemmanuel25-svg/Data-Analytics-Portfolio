**Project 01 - Hospital Patient Admission Trends**

**Objective:**
Analyze hospital patient admission data to identify trends
by time, age group, gender and admission type.

Tools Used
Microsoft Excel

Dataset
Healthcare Dataset - Kaggle
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/prasad22/healthcare-dataset

Key Findings
- Finding 1: Arthritis is the most common condition with 9,308 admissions
- Finding 2: Elective admissions lead at 18,655- most patients are planned not emergencies 
- Finding 3: Age 40-59 have the highest admissions at 16,408
